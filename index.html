Common questions

Good to know.

How often should my system be serviced?

Once a year for each side: your AC in the spring and your furnace or heat pump in the fall. Heat pumps do both jobs, so twice a year keeps them healthiest.

Why does my heat pump feel like it's blowing cool air?

Heat pump air is warm, but not as hot as a furnace's, so it can feel cool on your hand. It also runs short defrost cycles in freezing weather. If rooms aren't reaching the set temperature, give us a call.

How long does a new system install take?

Most replacements are finished in a single day. We protect your floors, haul away the old equipment, and walk you through the new system before we leave.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We accept cash, check and credit cards, and we offer financing on new systems. Ask about it when we quote your job.
